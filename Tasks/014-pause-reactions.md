# Task 014 — Pause Reactions (Do Not Disturb)

## Goal
One-click way to hush the pet temporarily.

## Requirements
- "Pause Reactions" toggle in the menu bar (and Settings).
- When paused: no cursor/typing reactions, stretch + Pomodoro timers do not fire
  nudges; the dog rests quietly. Dragging still works.
- Persisted; clearly indicated in the menu.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Toggling pause silences/restores all reactions live.
